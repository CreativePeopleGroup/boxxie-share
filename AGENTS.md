# Development rules

- Buttons within the same control group must have matching dimensions and alignment. Check both TV and phone layouts; retain visible keyboard/remote focus states.
- Refreshing or temporarily disconnecting an approved viewer must allow them to rejoin an open room. Preserve host approval, lock, removal and account permissions.
- Keep test results separate from claims of working physical-device audio. A successful generated beep alone does not prove third-party playback capture or background capture.

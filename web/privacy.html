Privacy

Last updated 24 October 2026

What Boxxie Share is

A personal, non-commercial app for watching your own films together with friends and talking on video while you watch.

What we keep

  • Your account: your name. If you sign in with Google, Facebook or email, also that email address and profile photo.
  • Your Boxxie code and authenticator lock: the code is kept only as a one-way scrambled copy, so nobody can read it back. If you switch on an authenticator app, its setup key is stored so the 6-digit numbers can be checked.
  • Your friends list: who you've added and who has accepted.
  • Room invites: kept only until the room closes (at most 12 hours).
  • Sign-in records: stored as one-way scrambled codes, so they can't be read back.
  • People you've blocked.
  • Reports you send: the reason, what you wrote, and the reported person's last few chat messages, so the report can be checked. Kept so the report can be checked and acted on.

What we never keep

  • Films, screens, video calls or voice. These go directly between devices, encrypted, and never pass through or stay on our server.
  • Chat messages. They're passed on live and not saved.
  • Passwords. There aren't any: you sign in with your Boxxie code, Google, Facebook or an emailed code.

Who it's shared with

Nobody. It's never sold or used for advertising. It's stored on Cloudflare, which runs the server, and sign-in emails are sent through our email provider.

Permissions on Android

Camera and microphone are only used when you join the video call. The Google Play version doesn't read your video library: you pick a film with Android's own file picker, and only that file is opened.

Deleting your account and data

In the app or on the website, sign in and open Friends → Delete my account. Your account, friends list and invites are deleted straight away. You can also email CONTACT-EMAIL and we'll delete it for you within 7 days.

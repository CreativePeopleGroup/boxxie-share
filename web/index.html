                        Watch on Apple TV

                        Your iPhone, iPad or Mac sends the film to the Apple TV with Screen Mirroring. Keep this device on the same Wi-Fi as the Apple TV.

                        1. iPhone or iPad: swipe down from the top-right corner to open Control Center.
                        2. Tap Screen Mirroring (two overlapping rectangles) and choose your Apple TV.
                        3. Come back here and tap Film full screen, then turn your phone sideways.

                        Mac: click Control Centre in the menu bar, then Screen Mirroring, and choose the Apple TV.

                        Talk on the call with headphones or a second phone, so the TV doesn't echo.

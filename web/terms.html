The short version

Boxxie Share is for watching your own videos with people you know. Be kind, only share what you have the right to share, and report anything that isn't OK.

What you can share

  • Only videos and screens you own or have permission to share, such as your own films, home videos and photos.
  • Don't use Boxxie Share to share films, TV, sport or streams from services that don't allow it, or to get around anyone's copy protection.

Zero tolerance

These aren't allowed anywhere in Boxxie Share: in shared videos, screens, video calls, names, photos or chat. Accounts that break them are banned.

  • Harassment, bullying, threats or encouraging violence.
  • Hate speech, or attacks on people for who they are.
  • Sexual content involving anyone under 18, or any content that puts a child at risk. We report this to the authorities.
  • Nudity or sexual content shared with anyone who hasn't agreed to see it.
  • Illegal content, scams or spam.

Staying safe

  • The host chooses who joins each room and can remove anyone at any time.
  • Tap a person in a room to block or report them. Blocked people can't send you friend requests, invite you, join your rooms or appear in your chat.
  • You can also report a problem from Friends → Report a problem or abuse.
  • Reports go straight to the people who run this Boxxie Share. We aim to look at them within 24 hours, and remove content or ban accounts that break these terms.
  • If someone is in immediate danger, call 999 (or your local emergency number).

Your account

You need to be 13 or older to use Boxxie Share. You can delete your account at any time from Friends → Delete my account. See the Privacy policy for what we keep.

No warranty

Boxxie Share is a free, personal project provided as it is. It may change or stop at any time.

Here's how Boxxie Share works on your Android TV. You can share anything that's on your TV with your friends, live.
Open Boxxie Share on the TV, choose TV screen, pick your picture quality, and press Start sharing.
Android will ask if Boxxie Share can record your screen. Choose Start now. You'll see this each time you start sharing.
Your room is ready. Send the room code to your friends by WhatsApp or iMessage, or they can scan the QR code.
When a friend asks to join, you'll see their name. Press Let in. Nobody gets in without your OK.
Now press Home on your remote and open any app, like your home videos. Boxxie Share keeps sharing in the background.
Your friends see and hear exactly what's on your TV, live, on their phone, laptop, iPad, or their own TV.
If someone new asks to join while you're watching, let them in from your paired phone. It also runs the video call, chat and remote.
When you're done, go back to Boxxie Share and press Stop sharing. Your friends stay in the room until you share again.
